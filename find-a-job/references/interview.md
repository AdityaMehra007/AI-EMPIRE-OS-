# Interview Preparation

## Story Bank Categories

Prepare stories for:

- Leadership or ownership
- Conflict or difficult stakeholder
- Ambiguous problem
- Technical depth or craft excellence
- Failure, learning, and resilience
- Collaboration across teams
- Customer or business impact
- Speed, prioritization, and tradeoffs

## STAR/CAR Structure

- Situation/Context: what was happening and why it mattered.
- Task/Challenge: what needed to be solved.
- Action: what the candidate personally did.
- Result: measurable or observable outcome.
- Reflection: what changed afterward or what the candidate learned.

## Answer Quality Checklist

- Opens with a direct answer.
- Uses a specific example, not general claims.
- Clarifies the user's individual contribution.
- Includes outcome and learning.
- Stays concise: usually 60-120 seconds for behavioral answers.

## Questions to Ask Interviewers

- What outcomes would define success in the first 90 days?
- What are the biggest challenges facing the team right now?
- How does the team make decisions and handle tradeoffs?
- What distinguishes strong performers in this role?
- What are the next steps in the process?
