# Resume Guidance

## Bullet Formula

Use: strong action verb + scope/context + method/tool + measurable or observable result.

Examples:

- Weak: Responsible for customer support.
- Strong: Resolved 45+ weekly customer tickets across billing and onboarding, reducing repeat escalations by documenting fixes in the team knowledge base.

If the user lacks metrics, ask for approximate scale, frequency, dollar value, time saved, quality improvement, stakeholder count, or before/after state. If no metric is available, use credible qualitative outcomes.

## Tailoring Checklist

- Match the target title when truthful.
- Put the most relevant experience in the top third.
- Mirror important keywords from the job description naturally.
- Use standard section names: Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications.
- Keep formatting simple for applicant tracking systems.
- Avoid tables, text boxes, images, unusual icons, and dense paragraphs when ATS compatibility matters.
- Remove irrelevant details that compete with the target role.

## Summary Pattern

`[Target role] with [years or depth] in [domain/tools], known for [differentiator] and [evidence/result].`

Do not fabricate years of experience or credentials.
