# Outreach Templates

## Referral Request

Hi [Name] — I saw [Company] is hiring for [Role]. My background in [relevant proof] lines up with [specific requirement]. Would you be open to referring me or pointing me to the right person? I can send a tailored resume and short summary.

## Recruiter Introduction

Hi [Name] — I’m exploring [target role] opportunities focused on [domain]. Recently, I [proof point]. I noticed you recruit for [team/company/role type]. If my background could fit current or upcoming openings, I’d appreciate a brief conversation.

## Informational Interview

Hi [Name] — I’m researching paths into [role/team/domain] and noticed your experience with [specific detail]. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat about your work and what strong candidates usually bring? No worries if now is not a good time.

## Follow-up

Hi [Name] — just following up on my note about [role/topic]. I’m still very interested because [specific reason]. Happy to send anything that would make it easier to evaluate fit.

## Adaptation Rules

- Keep messages under 120 words unless the user asks otherwise.
- Make the first sentence specific to the recipient.
- Include one clear ask.
- Do not pressure, exaggerate, or imply a relationship that does not exist.
