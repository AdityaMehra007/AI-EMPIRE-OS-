# Job Search Pipeline

## Fit Score

Score each role from 1-5:

1. Poor fit or major constraints.
2. Some overlap but weak evidence or undesirable constraints.
3. Plausible fit with gaps.
4. Strong fit with clear evidence.
5. Excellent fit, strong motivation, and referral or hiring signal.

## Weekly Cadence

- Review pipeline and choose top priorities.
- Tailor materials for high-fit roles.
- Send referral or recruiter outreach before applying when possible.
- Apply and log the date, materials used, and next action.
- Follow up after 5-7 business days when appropriate.
- Reserve time for interview practice and skill-gap work.

## Status Values

Use consistent statuses: Target, Contacted, Applied, Recruiter Screen, Interviewing, Take-home, Final, Offer, Rejected, Withdrawn, Dormant.
